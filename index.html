The Problem

Blindness is common, and current tools are costly or limited.

WHO reports at least 2.2 billion people have near or distance vision impairment, and at least 1 billion cases could have been prevented or are still unaddressed. Many existing aids are either expensive, socially intrusive, or only solve part of the mobility problem.

Common flaws in current solutions

White canes are affordable but mainly detect nearby obstacles and require constant manual scanning, while higher-end assistive devices can be effective yet prohibitively expensive for many users. Guide dog programs may provide life-changing support, but training, care, and access remain major barriers; the figure used here reflects a widely cited lifetime cost estimate for a guide dog program, while some Canadian organizations provide dogs free to users through sponsorship and funding.

How It Works

Four-stage pipeline from sensing to spoken guidance.

01 • Sense

LiDAR, RADAR, and camera capture the scene.

Depth and object cues are gathered continuously to understand obstacles, curbs, people, and walkable space.

02 • Process

Edge AI fuses the signals locally.

A compact processor merges sensor streams, reduces latency, and keeps the system responsive without relying on the cloud.

03 • Decode

Optional EEG/BCI selects targets by attention.

Dry electrodes feed the ADS1299, and a P300 model can identify the object the user is focusing on.

04 • Output

Directional audio guides movement.

Open-ear speakers deliver concise spatial prompts without blocking ambient awareness or conversations.

Technology

Built from practical hardware with a premium user experience.

LiDAR

Provides accurate distance mapping for walls, poles, stairs, and nearby hazards.

LiDAR detail

Great for geometry and obstacle range, helping Neuropoint understand what is physically in the way.

EEG / BCI

Dry electrodes and ADS1299 support P300 detection for thought-driven object focus.

BCI detail

An optional mode helps users choose between nearby objects without relying on touch or voice commands.

Edge AI

Runs scene understanding and audio decision-making directly on-device.

Edge AI detail

Local inference cuts latency, improves privacy, and keeps guidance working in noisy or offline settings.

Audio Output

Open-ear directional speakers keep the user aware of the real world.

Audio detail

Guidance stays spatial and discreet, reducing cognitive load while preserving environmental awareness.
